BUILT FOR STUDENTS

A student-built space, focused on learning.

UniHub is an independent academic platform. It has no religious, political, or other unrelated purpose or affiliation; it exists to support learning and student collaboration.

The platform is intended for practical, respectful exchange around university life: sharing relevant resources, keeping up with coursework, and helping students stay connected to their studies.

A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

Hi, I’m Mohamed Nejib Ben Hmida.

Third-year Ecological Engineering student · Bachelor’s degree in Big Data and Data Analysis

Like many students, I’ve seen how hard it can be to find the right course materials, keep up with what’s happening, and get clear information from classmates and colleagues. Too often, communication feels scattered just when we need it most.

I created UniHub to make that experience a little easier: one shared place to find your subjects, exchange useful resources, follow academic updates, and stay connected with the people learning alongside you.

This platform grew from a simple belief: when students can share knowledge and communicate more easily, we all have a better chance to thrive. I hope UniHub helps make your university journey feel more organized, connected, and supported.

Built with students, for the student journey.
